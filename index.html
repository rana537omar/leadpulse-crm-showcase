LeadPulse CRM

A CRM built for companies whose leads come from social media. Every Facebook and Instagram lead form, WhatsApp chat, LinkedIn or TikTok lead, Google Ads form and website enquiry lands in one place; the team works it through a simple pipeline; everyone's tasks stay on track; and the CEO follows the results from a live report link.

Features

Leads from every social account

  • Facebook & Instagram via one-click Meta login: Lead Ads forms and DMs in real time
  • WhatsApp Business chats become leads automatically
  • LinkedIn, TikTok, X, Snapchat, YouTube, Google Ads and website forms
  • Per-account leads, wins, win rate and 14-day trend

Lead management

  • One list with search, filters, CSV import and export
  • Duplicate detection by platform ID, email or phone
  • Automatic assignment to the sales team
  • Lead score, follow-ups and a full activity timeline
  • First-response time for every lead

Sales pipeline

  • Drag-and-drop stages from New to Won / Lost
  • Deal values per stage
  • Reasons recorded for every lost deal

Team tasks

  • Board and list views, owners, priorities, due dates
  • Overdue alerts
  • Automatic follow-up task for every new lead, closed when the lead is contacted

Reports for the CEO

  • Live report: sources, accounts, conversion, revenue, response times, team results
  • Read-only share link, no login, works on phones
  • Team note, expiry, view count and revoke
  • No customer contact details on the shared report

Team & security

  • Admin, Manager and Sales agent roles
  • Encrypted social account access and signed webhooks
  • Live notifications, dark mode, mobile layout
